# QALA: a mayor's term

Reading this as a welcoming city strategy game for first-time players: vivid Astana, tactile policy cards, an understandable public-service dilemma and visible consequences. Native React/CSS interface, IsoCity Canvas world, existing Manrope/Unbounded typography. Taste guidance applied contextually; this is a game, not a marketing dashboard.

DESIGN_VARIANCE 7 / MOTION_INTENSITY 5 / VISUAL_DENSITY 4.

## Experience contract

One clear action at each step: take office → understand a district → inspect a policy → fund it → see what changed → decide again → reflect on the term.

The main view holds a live city, a brief district story, three relevant options, budget, five decision slots and the official QoL. Detailed indicators, all fourteen policies, advisor, handbook and journal are secondary surfaces. A projected change never looks adopted. Applying is explicit; feedback shows actual metric changes and lets the player proceed at their pace. No auto-advancing dialogue, mandatory audio or fake citizen statistics.

## Visual direction

Rich blue-gray civic interface, golden action color, clean ivory policy paper, greener terrain and brighter blue river. Category colors identify a policy domain. Avoid the desaturated olive dashboard and crowded permanent panels. Cards use original building art; district identity comes from architecture and needs. Effects are short, purposeful and reduced-motion-aware.

## Gamification

Five decisions form a term. Earned milestones describe verified outcomes: resolve every critical indicator, activate a synergy, improve all five district scores. They add no score or money. Nothing alters the official eight-quarter horizon, budget, rules or deterministic arithmetic. Cosmetic time-of-day and movement stay separate from calculation.

## Completion criteria

Full intro/skip/continue; discover every policy and district; exact preview and adoption; retarget invalid choices; useful immediate feedback; undo; accessible secondary dialogs; structured AI with explicit fallback and no automatic adoption; a final term summary and detailed report; save/replay/export; RU/EN/KK; mobile and offline first launch. Test the actual first turn visually before expanding the rest.
